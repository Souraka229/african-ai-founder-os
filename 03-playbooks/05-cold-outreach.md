# Playbook 5 — Cold outreach (WhatsApp / LinkedIn / email)

Pairs with skill [`/write-cold-outreach`](../04-ai-skills/write-cold-outreach.md) and
[`../templates/cold-dm-whatsapp.md`](../templates/cold-dm-whatsapp.md) +
[`../templates/cold-email.md`](../templates/cold-email.md).

**Objective:** 30 qualified conversations in 21 days → 10 demos → 3-5 clients or design partners.

**Prerequisites:** a precise ICP (sector, size, role, country, trigger), a list of 150 target
accounts (Apollo/Clay/manual), 3 message variants.

## Steps

1. **D1-D2 — Build the list** (150 contacts, verify emails, note a personal trigger for the first 30).
2. **D2 — Write 3 sequences** (`/write-cold-outreach`): WhatsApp (short, optional voice note),
   LinkedIn (connect + note + follow-up), email (3 touches).
3. **D3-D17 — Send 10 contacts/day/channel**, personalise the first line. Follow up at D+3 and D+7.
4. **Ongoing — Reply in < 2h**, offer a direct Cal.com slot.
5. **D18-D21 — Run the demos**, offer "design partner" status (free/discounted access for feedback + testimonial).

## Template — cold WhatsApp (restaurant sector)

```
Hi [First name], I'm Souraka, building [Product] in Cotonou.
I saw [concrete detail: your page, your evening WhatsApp queue].
We help restaurants like yours [outcome: stop losing evening orders].
Got 15 min this week to tell me how you handle it today?
(not selling — I mostly want to understand your current setup)
```

## Template — cold email (3 touches)

```
T1 (day 0) — Subject: [outcome] for [company]?
  1 line personalisation · 1 line problem · 1 line proof · 1 soft question.
T2 (day 3) — "Bumping this — one example: [one-line mini case study]."
T3 (day 7) — "I'll stop here. If [problem] ever becomes a priority, I'm around. A useful link meanwhile: [repo resource]."
```

## Success metrics

Reply rate ≥ 15% (WhatsApp often 30%+) · ≥ 30 conversations · ≥ 10 demos · ≥ 3 design partners.

## Traps

Too-long pitch · no personalisation · talking product before problem · single touch · sending
from a non-warmed inbox (spam) · ignoring local time zones/hours.
