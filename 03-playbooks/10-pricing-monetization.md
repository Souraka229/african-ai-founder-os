# Playbook 10 — Pricing & first revenue ($1k → $10k MRR)

Pairs with skills [`/optimize-pricing`](../04-ai-skills/optimize-pricing.md) and
[`/research-competitors`](../04-ai-skills/research-competitors.md), and
[`../templates/pricing-page.md`](../templates/pricing-page.md).

**Objective:** 0 → $1k MRR in 30 days, then aim for $10k MRR in 90 days.

**Prerequisites:** ≥ 100 active users OR ≥ 10 happy design partners; understanding of the "job"
(JTBD) and the value created (time/money).

## Steps

1. **Week 1 — Price research:** Van Westendorp on 30 users (`/optimize-pricing`) + analysis of 5
   competitors (`/research-competitors`) + "what monthly budget does this deserve for you?".
2. **Week 1 — Build 3 tiers** (Good-Better-Best), the middle one is the target, in **local
   currency** + a monthly mobile-money option. Anchor value ("= X recovered orders").
3. **Week 2 — Turn on billing** (Paystack/Flutterwave subscriptions or the adapter). Pricing page.
4. **Week 2-3 — Convert design partners:** 1-to-1 chat, "early adopter" offer (-30-50% for life)
   for an annual commitment or a testimonial.
5. **Week 3-4 — Paid onboarding:** the paywall comes *after* the value moment (activation), not before.
6. **Month 2-3 — Expansion:** upsell (volume, seats, multi-site), cut churn (mobile-money payment
   failure recovery is issue #1), ask for reviews.

## Template — 3 tiers

```
Starter (monthly, mobile money) — [local price]: 1 location, [volume cap], WhatsApp support.
Pro (recommended) — [local price]: up to 3 locations, [volume ×5], analytics, no branding.
Business — [local price or "contact us"]: multi-site, API, dedicated onboarding, SLA.
Annual: -2 months. Early adopter: -40% for life (first 30 sign-ups).
```

## Success metrics

$1k MRR by D30 · ≥ 20 paying customers · monthly churn < 7% · ≥ 1 upsell · $10k MRR trajectory by D90.

## Traps

Pricing in USD only · forcing annual (cash flow) · paywall before value · ignoring recurring
payment failures (mobile money = many retries) · too many tiers · unlimited-time discounts.
