# Playbook 6 — Launch (Product Hunt / Hacker News / X)

Pairs with [`../templates/launch-checklist.md`](../templates/launch-checklist.md) and
[`../06-launch-virality/post-templates.md`](../06-launch-virality/post-templates.md).

**Objective:** a coordinated visibility spike → Product Hunt top 3, or HN front page, or an X
thread > 100k views → backlinks + signups + stars.

**Prerequisites (D-7):** product page ready, 15s GIF, 5 screenshots, FAQ, a written founder story,
a list of 50-100 pre-briefed supporters, a warm PH account, an asset kit.

## Plan D-7 → D+1

| Day | Actions |
|---|---|
| D-7 | Brief 80 supporters (personal DM, no spam group). Prepare 3 tweets + 1 thread + LinkedIn post + "Show HN" text. Schedule the newsletter. |
| D-3 | Test the page on mobile/3G. Re-read the founder story. Prepare answers to objections. |
| D-1 | Confirm 30 supporters for the first hour. Schedule the PH post for 00:01 PT (09:01 UTC). Sleep. |
| **D0** | 00:01 PT: PH live. 08:00 ET: "Show HN". Then X thread + LinkedIn. Reply to **every** comment in < 15 min for 12h. Post in 5 communities. Newsletter in the morning. |
| D+1 | Thank people by name. Publish the recap (day's numbers). Turn feedback into issues. Follow up with the undecided. |

## Template — "Show HN"

```
Show HN: African AI Founder OS – an open-source OS to go from idea to $10k MRR with AI

I'm building RESTAFY (restaurant SaaS) from Benin. Every week I hit the same wall:
which tool, which grant, which payment provider works *here*? So I turned my playbook
into a repo: 10 executable playbooks, 10 Claude Code skills, a pre-vetted stack, and an
Africa layer (mobile-money providers by country, grants, accelerators, regulations).

It's opinionated and Africa-first but the playbooks/skills are useful anywhere.
Would love feedback on the skills and what's missing for your country. [link]
```

## Success metrics

PH top 5 / HN front page ≥ 2h / thread > 50k views · +300 stars on the day · +200 emails · ≥ 5 backlinks.

## Traps

Launching on a Friday/Monday · explicitly asking for "upvotes" (bannable on PH/HN) · disappearing
after posting · page not mobile-ready · no founder story (the narrative is half the result).
