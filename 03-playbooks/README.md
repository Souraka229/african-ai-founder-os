# Part 3 — Playbooks

Ten one-page playbooks. Read in 5 min, run in days. Format everywhere:
**Objective → Prerequisites → Steps → Template → Success metrics → Traps.**

**Run order:** 1 → 2 → 3 → (4, 5, 6 in parallel) → 7 once you have traction → 8 & 9 ongoing → 10 when you go for revenue.

| # | Playbook | Objective | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Validate the idea](01-validate-idea.md) | 10 interviews + 100 waitlist + go/no-go | 14 d |
| 2 | [Build the MVP with AI](02-build-mvp-7-days.md) | Usable product in production | 7 d |
| 3 | [GTM 0 → 1000 users](03-gtm-0-to-1000.md) | First 1000 users | 90 d |
| 4 | [Content marketing](04-content-marketing.md) | Organic traffic engine | ongoing |
| 5 | [Cold outreach](05-cold-outreach.md) | 30 qualified conversations | 21 d |
| 6 | [Launch (PH / HN / X)](06-launch.md) | Visibility spike + backlinks | 1 d (7 d prep) |
| 7 | [Funding (grants → pre-seed)](07-funding.md) | $5-50k non-dilutive or pre-seed | 60-120 d |
| 8 | [Community building](08-community-building.md) | 500 active members | 90 d |
| 9 | [Open-source contribution](09-open-source-contribution.md) | 25 contributors on your repo | ongoing |
| 10 | [Pricing & monetization](10-pricing-monetization.md) | First $1k → $10k MRR | 30-90 d |

Each pairs with an [AI skill](../04-ai-skills/) and, where relevant, a [template](../templates/).
Full versions are also inside [`../MASTER-DOC.md`](../MASTER-DOC.md#part-3--playbooks--templates).
