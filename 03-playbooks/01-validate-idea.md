# Playbook 1 — Validate the idea (0 → 10 interviews → 100 waitlist)

Pairs with skill [`/validate-idea`](../04-ai-skills/validate-idea.md) and
[`../templates/interview-guide.md`](../templates/interview-guide.md).

**Objective:** a fact-based go/no-go in 14 days — 10 Mom-Test interviews, 100 waitlist signups,
≥ 5 people who agree to pay / pre-order.

**Prerequisites:** the idea in one sentence ("I help [WHO] to [DO X] so that [OUTCOME]"), access
to ~30 people in the target segment (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, communities), 2h/day.

## Steps

1. **D1 — Write the hypothesis.** Fill the canvas: segment, problem, current alternative,
   *riskiest assumption* (RAT). Example (RESTAFY): "Cotonou restaurants lose orders because they
   run WhatsApp by hand → RAT: they *want* to change, not just complain."
2. **D2 — Prepare the interview guide** (Mom Test): questions about the past and facts only. See template below.
3. **D2 — Build the waitlist:** Tally or Framer — one-line promise + email + one qualifying
   question + "ready to pay X?" checkbox.
4. **D3-D10 — Run 10 interviews** (20-30 min). Record them (Fathom/Granola). One per day minimum.
   After each: note the harshest verbatim, the current workaround, any budget mentioned.
5. **D3-D12 — Recruit the waitlist:** 3 posts (LinkedIn, X, 2 WhatsApp groups), 20 personalised
   DMs, 1 message in 2 communities. Target 100 signups.
6. **D11 — Pre-sale test:** ask the 10 interviewees (and hot waitlist) to reserve a spot with a
   token deposit (mobile money) or an LOI. Target ≥ 5 concrete "yes".
7. **D13 — Synthesis:** code the insights (the skill does this), fill the decision grid.
8. **D14 — Decision:** Go / Pivot / No-go against the thresholds below.

## Template — Mom Test interview guide

```
1. Tell me about the last time you [did the task tied to the problem].
2. What was painful about that? (let the silence sit)
3. What did you do to work around it? (tool, person, hack)
4. Roughly how much time / money did that cost you?
5. Have you looked for a solution? What did you try? Why didn't it stick?
6. If you had a magic wand for this, what would it do? (just to explore)
7. Who else has this problem and should I talk to?
FORBIDDEN: "Would you use...", "Would you pay...", "Do you think you'd like...".
```

## Success metrics (go thresholds)

| Signal | No-go | Grey zone | Go |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interviews where the problem is "top 3" for the persona | < 3/10 | 3-5/10 | ≥ 6/10 |
| Costly existing workaround (time/money) | rare | sometimes | ≥ 6/10 |
| Waitlist in 12 days | < 40 | 40-99 | ≥ 100 |
| Pre-sales / LOIs | 0-1 | 2-4 | ≥ 5 |

## Traps

Interviewing friends · asking hypothetical questions · pitching instead of listening · mistaking
politeness for interest · a waitlist with no qualifying question (vanity number).
