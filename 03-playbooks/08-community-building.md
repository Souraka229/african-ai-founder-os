# Playbook 8 — Community building (Discord / WhatsApp / GitHub Discussions)

**Objective:** 500 active members (post/react at least once a month) in 90 days, with 10 regular
content contributors.

**Prerequisites:** a clear "why we gather" ("African founders shipping with AI"), 3-5 channels max
at first, 5 engaged founding members.

## Steps

1. **D1 — Minimal structure:** channels `#introduce-yourself`, `#ideas-and-feedback`, `#wins`,
   `#africa-tools`, `#grants-and-deals`. Short rules. Welcome bot.
2. **D1-D14 — Seeding (Cold Start):** invite 50 people 1-to-1, no public link. Post one useful
   thing yourself every day (a tool, a grant closing soon, a question).
3. **Weeks 3-6 — Rituals:** "Friday win", monthly "office hours" (you + 1 guest), "deal of the
   week" (a credit/grant). Spotlight members.
4. **Weeks 6-12 — Delegate:** appoint 3-5 moderators/ambassadors (by country if possible), give
   them a role and visibility.
5. **Ongoing — Repo ↔ community loop:** every new repo resource is announced in the community;
   every good discussion becomes a PR.

## Success metrics

≥ 500 members · ≥ 25% monthly active · ≥ 10 non-founder posts/day · ≥ 3 autonomous ambassadors ·
new-member response time < 12h.

## Traps

Opening 20 empty channels · going public too early (ghost-town effect) · not moderating
spam/scams (common in "African founders" groups) · centralising everything on yourself.
