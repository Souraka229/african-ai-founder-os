# Playbook 3 — GTM: 0 → 1000 users in 90 days

Pairs with skill [`/create-gtm`](../04-ai-skills/create-gtm.md).

**Objective:** 1000 signed-up users, ≥ 100 weekly active, via 2 proven channels (Bullseye).

**Prerequisites:** MVP live, activation event instrumented, the Playbook 1 waitlist.

## Steps

1. **Week 1 — Convert the waitlist.** Email + WhatsApp every signup; hand-onboard the first 20
   (call / screen-share). Target 50-100 users.
2. **Weeks 2-4 — Test 5 channels** at small dose (Bullseye): (a) sector WhatsApp groups,
   (b) LinkedIn/X content, (c) cold outreach (Playbook 5), (d) one micro-influencer partnership,
   (e) niche SEO/directory. Equal time each, 2 weeks, measure cost/user and activation per channel.
3. **Week 5 — Double down on channel #1**, keep #2 as support, cut the other 3.
4. **Weeks 6-10 — Content loop** (Playbook 4): 3 pieces/week, each with a product CTA, each
   recycled into 3 formats.
5. **Weeks 8-12 — Referral loop:** add an "invite & get X" mechanic fit for your context
   (credit, free month, priority access). Measure the coefficient.
6. **Week 12 — Review:** 1000 users? If not, which link breaks (acquisition, activation,
   retention) → one week of targeted fixing.

## Template — channel test table

```
Channel | Hours invested | Users | Cost $ | Activation rate | Verdict (double / support / cut)
```

## Success metrics

1000 signups · ≥ 10% activation · ≥ 100 WAU · sustainable CAC (< a user's 3-month value) ·
one channel with a steady slope.

## Traps

Launching 10 channels half-heartedly · judging a channel in 3 days · optimising acquisition
while retention leaks · skipping hand-onboarding of the first 50 (that's where you learn).
