# Playbook 4 — Content marketing: 1 → 10 → 100

Pairs with skills [`/plan-content-30-days`](../04-ai-skills/plan-content-30-days.md) and
[`/create-viral-hook`](../04-ai-skills/create-viral-hook.md), and
[`../templates/content-calendar.md`](../templates/content-calendar.md).

**Objective:** an organic traffic engine producing ≥ 500 visits/month to the product by M+3,
≥ 3000 by M+6.

**Prerequisites:** 5 topics where you have a unique angle (your field, your numbers, your country).

## System (repeat every week)

1. **1 pillar piece/week** — 800-1500-word article OR 3-6 min video OR teardown. Always:
   real problem → method → quantified result → product CTA.
2. **Atomise into 3-4** — 1 X thread, 1 LinkedIn post, 1 short (Reel/Short), 1 community message.
3. **Distribute** — your account + 2 communities + 1 partner newsletter (mention swap) + Reddit/HN if relevant.
4. **Recycle at M+1** — reformat last month's best 4.
5. **Light SEO** — 1 long-tail keyword per pillar, title + H2 + FAQ, internal links to the product.

## Template — pillar article structure

```
Title: How to [concrete outcome] in [country/context] (with numbers)
Hook (2 lines): the tension + the promise.
Context: why this is hard here specifically.
Method: 3-5 numbered steps, screenshots, commands.
Result: before/after numbers, PostHog screenshot.
What I'd do differently.
CTA: "Full playbook + templates: [repo] · Try [product]: [link]"
```

## Success metrics

≥ 4 pillars/month · ≥ 1 "hit" (>5k views) per month from M+2 · organic product traffic up 3 months running.

## Traps

Generic "10 productivity tips" content (no angle) · publishing without distributing · switching
format every week · no CTA · never recycling.
