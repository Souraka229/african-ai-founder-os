# Start here — 15 minutes to a first result

You don't read this repo front to back. You pick where you are and take one action.

---

## 💡 "I have an idea (or three)"

1. Open [`../03-playbooks/01-validate-idea.md`](../03-playbooks/01-validate-idea.md). Skim the 8 steps.
2. Copy [`../04-ai-skills/validate-idea.md`](../04-ai-skills/validate-idea.md) into Claude Code.
3. Run it:
   ```
   /validate-idea
   idea: <your one sentence>
   segment: <who exactly>
   country: <your market>
   ```
4. You now have: a Mom-Test interview guide, a waitlist plan tailored to your country, and a go/no-go grid.
5. **Next 14 days:** 10 interviews + 100 waitlist signups + 5 pre-sales. Then decide.

## 🛠️ "I have an MVP, I need users"

1. Open [`../03-playbooks/03-gtm-0-to-1000.md`](../03-playbooks/03-gtm-0-to-1000.md).
2. Run skill [`create-gtm`](../04-ai-skills/create-gtm.md) → a 30/60/90-day plan with 5 channels to test.
3. Check [`../02-tools-stack/`](../02-tools-stack/) for analytics + outreach + WhatsApp tooling.
4. **This week:** instrument your activation event, convert your waitlist, start 5 channel tests.

## 💰 "I need money (without giving away equity yet)"

1. Open [`../05-africa/grants-by-country.md`](../05-africa/grants-by-country.md) and [`accelerators.md`](../05-africa/accelerators.md).
2. Check [`../02-tools-stack/cloud-credits.md`](../02-tools-stack/cloud-credits.md) — stack AWS + Google + Azure credits.
3. Run skill [`generate-pitch-deck`](../04-ai-skills/generate-pitch-deck.md) (`type: grant`).
4. **This month:** list 15 opportunities, build reusable assets, submit 3 applications/week.

## 🚀 "I'm about to launch something"

1. [`../06-launch-virality/launch-plan.md`](../06-launch-virality/launch-plan.md) — the J-7 → J+90 plan.
2. [`../templates/launch-checklist.md`](../templates/launch-checklist.md) — print it.
3. [`../06-launch-virality/post-templates.md`](../06-launch-virality/post-templates.md) — Show HN, X thread, LinkedIn, Reddit, community.
4. **Rule:** launch Tuesday, Hacker News first, personal story, be in the comments for 12 hours.

## 🧠 "I want the whole thing"

Read [`../MASTER-DOC.md`](../MASTER-DOC.md) — the full research & strategy document (~26k words, 10 parts + appendices).

- **Community chat** — link in the [README](../README.md). Introduce yourself, say your country.
- **Contribute in 1 PR** — add your country's payment provider or a fresh grant. See [`../CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md). First review in < 24h.
- **Document your journey** — if you build with this, open a Discussion. Your case study helps the next founder.
