# Grant application — reusable answers

Write these once, then tailor the **intro** and **impact** per program.

## Problem (150-200 words)
Concrete, [market]-specific. Name the people affected, the frequency, the cost. One anecdote.
Avoid "huge market" language — show you've talked to real users.

## Solution (150 words)
The "one-thing" + how AI makes it feasible now for a small team. One sentence on what it is NOT.

## Traction (bullets + numbers)
- Users / WAU / signups:
- Revenue / MRR / LOIs:
- Retention proof (one metric):
- Partnerships / pilots:
- What we shipped in the last 90 days:

## Market (bottom-up)
[# target customers in market] × [realistic annual price] = [SOM]. Then SAM (region), TAM (Africa).
Source your numbers.

## Team (100 words)
Names, roles, and the **local unfair advantage** (you're from here, you speak the language, you
have distribution). Time commitment (full-time?).

|---|---|---|---|
| Product | 50% | | |

## Impact (tailor per program)
Jobs created / supported · founders or SMEs served · inclusion (women, rural, informal sector) ·
local ecosystem contribution (open source, mentoring, this repo). Give numbers and a 12-month target.

## Milestones (6 & 12 months)
- 6 months: [MRR], [active users], [1 retention proof]
- 12 months: [MRR], [expansion metric], [next market]

## Risks & mitigations
3 rows: demand, execution, regulatory/FX.
