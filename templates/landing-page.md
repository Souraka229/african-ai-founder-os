# Landing page — wireframe + copy

Keep it one screen of value before any scroll. Loads fast on 3G (< 1.5s, images compressed).

## Above the fold
- **Headline:** [the outcome, in the customer's words] — max 8 words.
- **Subhead:** [who it's for] + [how it works in 1 line].
- **Primary CTA:** [Join the waitlist] / [Start free] — one button, high contrast.
- **Visual:** product screenshot or 10s loop. Real, not a stock illustration.
- **Trust line:** "Used by [N] restaurants in [city]" / "Built in [country]".

## Section 2 — The problem (they should nod)
3 bullets of the pain, in concrete local terms.

## Section 3 — How it works
3 steps, one icon + one line each. Show the "landing → WhatsApp → mobile money" flow if relevant.

## Section 4 — Proof
2-3 short testimonials (name, business, city, one sentence) OR a before/after number.

## Section 5 — Pricing teaser
"From [local price]/month. Mobile money. Cancel anytime." → link to full pricing.

## Section 6 — FAQ (4-5)
Payment methods · offline/data use · setup time · support channel · data safety.

## Footer
Contact (WhatsApp), [repo/OSS link], socials, [country] 🇧🇯.

## Waitlist form (Tally/Fillout)
- Email (required)
- [Qualifying question — e.g. "How many locations?"]
- "Would you pay [currency][price]/month for this?" (Yes / Maybe / No)
