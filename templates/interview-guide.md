# Interview guide (Mom Test) + coding grid

## Before the call
- Segment of this person:
- What I want to learn (1 sentence):
- My riskiest assumption I'm testing:

## Questions (past & facts only)
1. Tell me about the last time you [did the task tied to the problem].
2. What was painful about that? *(let the silence sit)*
3. What did you do to work around it? (tool, person, hack)
4. Roughly how much time / money did that cost you?
5. Have you looked for a solution? What did you try? Why didn't it stick?
6. If you had a magic wand for this, what would it do? *(explore only)*
7. Who else has this problem and should I talk to?

**Forbidden:** "Would you use…", "Would you pay…", "Do you think you'd like…", any future hypothetical.
