# /plan-content-30-days

**Pairs with** [Playbook 4](../03-playbooks/04-content-marketing.md) and
[`../templates/content-calendar.md`](../templates/content-calendar.md).

## Prompt (paste this)

```
You run content for a startup in traction phase. Build a 30-day editorial calendar realistic
for a solo founder.

INPUT
Product: {product}
Audience: {audience}
Unique angles (my field, my numbers, my country): {angles}
Channels: {channels}
Sustainable cadence: {cadence}   (e.g. 1 pillar + 3 atoms / week)
Link to promote: {link}

PRODUCE
1. 4 PILLAR PIECES (1/week): title, format, promise, 5-point outline, CTA, long-tail keyword,
   "why it can break through".
2. DAY-BY-DAY CALENDAR (D1..D30): date | channel | format | topic | CTA | estimated time.
   Include the atoms (threads, posts, shorts, community messages) derived from the pillars.
3. 10 REUSABLE HOOK IDEAS (point to /create-viral-hook to expand them).
4. RECYCLING LOOP: what to repost/reformat at D31-D45.
5. METRICS: what to check weekly (reach, {link} clicks, signups attributed via Dub) + "working
   / adjust" thresholds.
6. ANTI-BURNOUT RULE: what to cut if a week overflows (keep the pillar, sacrifice 2 atoms).

Adapt tone and references to {audience}. No generic "productivity" content.
```

## Traps

Too-ambitious cadence → abandoned by D10. No proprietary angle → indistinguishable content.
