# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers — pledge to make participation in African AI Founder OS
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Encouraged:** friendly and patient communication, useful and sourced contributions,
gracefully accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the community,
showing empathy toward other members.

**Not tolerated:**
- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- **Spam, scams, "guaranteed funding" pitches, unsolicited crypto/"investor" DMs, or
  promotional messages** — this community is a frequent target and we act fast.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainers at **conduct@africanaifounder.dev** (or a DM to a maintainer). All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that violate this
Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviors they
deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
