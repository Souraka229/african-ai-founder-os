## The 10 patterns that win stars

1. Positioning in 5 words ("the OS for African founders who ship with AI").
2. The README does the work: promise + visual proof + how-to-start in < 7s.
3. Trivial contribution (add a line / a YAML / a country).
4. Identity or anti-pain angle.
5. Concentrated launch: HN first, Tue-Thu 8-10 ET, personal narrative.
6. A light landing page alongside the repo.
7. Multilingual (FR+EN from day one — a natural edge here).
8. Post-launch rhythm: weekly drops, visible changelog, contributors spotlighted.
9. Contribution loop: each contributor brings their audience.
10. Continuous social proof: star-history graph, testimonials, "used by".

## The 5 things that kill it

Asking for stars/upvotes · launching Mon/Fri · posting then vanishing · scope too broad ·
buying stars · ignored PRs · zero drops after launch · no country angle · centralising on
yourself · skipping English · dead links in the Africa layer.
