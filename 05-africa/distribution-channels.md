# Distribution channels by geography

| Channel | Where it dominates | Founder use |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp** | Everywhere (West Africa ++), B2C & B2SMB | Sales, support, onboarding, broadcast (Cloud API) |
| **Facebook / Groups** | West & Central Africa, 30+ | Sector communities, marketplace, cheap ads |
| **TikTok** | Youth, urban — KE/NG/CI/SN/ZA | "Before/after" product demos, local creators |
| **Instagram** | Urban, lifestyle, restaurants/fashion | Product showcase, DM sales |
| **Telegram** | Devs, crypto, tech communities | Founder/dev groups, announcement channels |
| **LinkedIn** | B2B, corporate — Nigeria/SA/Kenya | Enterprise outbound, hiring, thought leadership |
| **X (Twitter)** | Tech Twitter NG/KE, diaspora | Launch, threads, VC/founder networking |
| **Radio / OOH / field** | Peri-urban, B2SMB | Door-to-door, distributor partnerships, markets |
| **USSD / SMS** | Non-smartphones, rural | Onboarding without data (Africa's Talking) |
| **Podcasts / YouTube (Africa tech)** | Diaspora + urban | Founder interviews, backlinks |

**Practical read:** for most Africa B2C/B2SMB SaaS the real funnel is
**landing → WhatsApp → mobile money**. Build for that first.
