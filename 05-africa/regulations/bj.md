# Benin — regulation checklist (BJ)

> Not legal advice. Starter notes for Souraka's home market — **needs local-lawyer confirmation
> and dated sources on every line.** Contributions from Benin founders very welcome.

**Last checked:** 2026-09 (partial) · **Maintainer:** _open — claim it_

## Company setup
- Common structure: SARL (à associé unique possible) or SAS.
- One-stop shop: **APIeX** (Agence de Promotion des Investissements et des Exports) — guichet unique. `source: apiex.bj — verify`
- Cost/time: reported low cost and a few days via APIeX — **confirm current figures**.
- Foreign ownership: generally allowed; confirm sector restrictions.

## "Startup" status
- **Loi sur les startups au Bénin** exists in principle — check the current status and the
  *implementing decree* (décret d'application) via **ASIN** (Agence des Systèmes d'Information
  et du Numérique). `source: asin.bj — verify`
- Benefits to confirm: tax easing, access to public support, procurement.

## Data protection
- Authority: **APDP** (Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel), under the
  *Code du Numérique*. Controller declaration/authorisation regime applies. `source: apdp.bj — verify`
- Consent, purpose limitation, and cross-border transfer rules apply.

## Fintech / payments
- Regulator: **BCEAO** (UEMOA). Most startups operate under a licensed PSP's cover
  (KkiaPay, FedaPay, CinetPay) rather than holding their own licence early.
- A dedicated e-money / aggregator licence becomes relevant at scale — confirm thresholds with BCEAO.

## Tax
- VAT (TVA) standard rate ~18% (UEMOA norm) — confirm current rate + registration threshold.
- Check withholding taxes on cross-border services and any e-invoicing rollout.

## Moving money
- UEMOA FX controls apply (XOF, fixed peg to EUR). Grant disbursement and fundraising in foreign
  currency need proper documentation — plan for delays.

## Employment
- Code du Travail applies; social contributions via **CNSS**.
- For international contractors/hires, EOR (Deel, Remote) is common.
